# Code of Conduct

OpenClasp contributors must communicate respectfully, review ideas on their technical merits, and
help keep participation safe and constructive.

Harassment, discrimination, threats, deliberate disruption, doxxing, and disclosure of private or
security-sensitive information are not acceptable.

Project maintainers may edit or remove contributions and restrict participation when conduct harms
the project or its community. Report conduct concerns privately to
[@aryaamin](https://github.com/aryaamin). Do not use a public issue for sensitive reports.
